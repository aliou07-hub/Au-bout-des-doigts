Our Story

An Italian table at the heart of N'Djamena

Au Bout Des Doigts brings together a wood-fired pizza oven, refined Italian and French-inspired plates, and a relaxed lounge bar under one roof. Guests dine inside or out on the garden terrace, with attentive table service from lunch through late night.

  • Wood-fired pizza oven
  • Outdoor garden terrace
  • Full bar & wine list
  • Takeaway available
  • Vegetarian & vegan options
  • Wheelchair accessible
